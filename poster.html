Introduction

Large Language Models (LLMs) are widely used in critical applications, yet they often generate confident but incorrect responses. Our project proposes a multi-granular uncertainty quantification (MGUQ) framework that estimates uncertainty at the token, claim, and semantic levels to provide reliable and interpretable confidence scores.

Problem Statement

LLMs can produce plausible but incorrect or hallucinated outputs with high confidence. Existing uncertainty estimation methods are often limited to a single granularity and fail to capture uncertainty comprehensively across different levels of understanding.

Goal

To develop a calibrated framework that quantifies uncertainty at multiple granularities (token, claim, and semantic) and aggregates them into a reliable confidence score for each LLM response.

Key Objectives
  • Capture uncertainty at token, claim, and semantic levels.
  • Aggregate multi-granular uncertainty into a single calibrated score.
  • Evaluate and calibrate using standard UQ benchmarks.
  • Provide interpretable visualizations and reports.
